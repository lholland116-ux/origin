# LVT-CAPA-QUAL-001 — Controlled Qualification Verification Instruction

**Document ID:** LVT-CAPA-QUAL-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** RELEASED
**Document Owner:** LVTChat CAPA Owner
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-11
**Related CAPA:** CAPA-000008

## 1. Purpose

This instruction establishes the controlled verification requirements for LVTChat qualification activities where a second-person verification step is required.

## 2. Scope

This instruction applies to controlled qualification executions performed for LVTChat where the approved qualification activity requires independent or second-person verification of a result, decision, record, configuration, or completion step.

## 3. Roles

### Qualification Executor
Performs the qualification activity and records the result and supporting evidence.

### Second-Person Verifier
Reviews the required verification item independently of the original recorded result where second-person verification is required.

The verifier must not alter the original execution record in place.

## 4. When Verification Is Required

Second-person verification is required when the approved qualification instruction, protocol, test step, CAPA action, or acceptance criterion explicitly identifies verification as required.

Verification must occur before the affected qualification step is considered complete.

## 5. What Must Be Verified

The verifier must confirm, as applicable:

1. the required activity was performed;
2. the recorded result corresponds to the objective evidence;
3. the applicable acceptance criterion was correctly applied;
4. required supporting evidence is present and attributable;
5. required deviations or exceptions are identified;
6. the completion decision is consistent with the controlled instruction.

## 6. Permitted Verification Methods

Verification may include one or more of the following:

- direct review of the controlled execution record;
- comparison of the recorded result with objective evidence;
- review of system-generated records or logs;
- review of screenshots or controlled test evidence;
- direct observation when observation is the approved evidence method;
- confirmation against an authoritative controlled source.

The method used must be identifiable from the verification record.

## 7. Acceptance Decision

The verifier records one of the following outcomes:

- **Verified / Acceptable**
- **Verification Failed**
- **Verification Incomplete**

A qualification step requiring verification must not be considered successfully completed unless the outcome is **Verified / Acceptable**.

## 8. Required Verification Record

The verification record must identify:

- the item or step verified;
- the verifier;
- the verification date or timestamp;
- the verification method;
- the evidence or source reviewed;
- the verification outcome;
- any applicable comment, exception, or discrepancy.

The original execution record and the verification record must remain attributable and traceable.

## 9. Failed Verification

If verification fails:

1. the affected qualification step must not be accepted as complete;
2. the discrepancy must be documented;
3. applicable correction, deviation, investigation, or re-execution must be initiated;
4. subsequent verification must remain separately traceable and must not overwrite the failed verification record.

## 10. Missing Verification

If required verification has not been performed, the affected qualification step remains incomplete.

Missing verification must not be inferred, backdated, or automatically supplied by AI or software.

## 11. AI Boundary

AI may provide advisory assistance but must not:

- perform the required second-person verification;
- create objective verification evidence;
- approve a verification result;
- overwrite a human verification decision;
- automatically transition a controlled qualification step.

## 12. Records and Retention

Qualification execution records, verification records, evidence references, deviations, and applicable approval/release records must remain attributable, traceable, and protected from uncontrolled overwrite.
